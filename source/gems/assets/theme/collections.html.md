# Collection colors

Collection colors distinguish Lesli product areas, categories, charts, avatars, tabs, and accents. They support the product hierarchy but do not replace the application primary color.

Each palette provides five variants. Prefer 100 for soft backgrounds, 500 for accents, and 700–900 for text or strong iconography.
